# Product: UniFix

UniFix lets college students report what's broken on campus in plain language. AI classifies each
report, asks only for the details still missing, merges duplicates into one co-signed ticket, ranks
by severity × reach, and routes it to the one staff team that fixes it. Ari, a small robot assistant,
shows history and safe quick fixes. Hackachusetts track: **Civic Action**.

Rules that always apply:
- **Safety beats everything.** Emergency wording shows Call 911 + Campus Police instantly, before any
  AI runs; the AI can raise an emergency but never close one. Never auto-dial. Hazards get the campus
  police number and never get quick fixes.
- **Public reports never name a person.** Those go privately to exactly one office.
- **Anonymous to students, visible to staff.** Students see counts, not names.
- **Staff see only their own area** (Facilities sees Facilities, IT sees IT…).
- **Sort by priority (severity × reach)**, never by upvotes alone.
- **Ask the fewest questions:** max 3 follow-ups, location first, chips over typing, "No specific place" allowed.
- **Junk stays out:** spam, jokes, prize/money claims and keyboard noise are rejected; the text is kept.
- Plain words in every UI string; accessible by keyboard and screen reader; tiers are text + color.
