## Recording tips
- Record the voice over the video in any editor (iMovie, CapCut, QuickTime + iMovie). Keep music off unless it is royalty-free and you have the right to use it.
- Upload to **YouTube and set it to Public** (the rules say the video must be publicly visible). Put the link in the submission form and in `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_URL` so the site's **Watch the Demo** button plays it.
- The video shows MIT only as a seeded demo campus. To avoid any third-party trademark in the voice-over, say "a demo campus" instead of "MIT".
